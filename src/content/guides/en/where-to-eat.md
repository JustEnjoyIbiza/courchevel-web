## Michelin-starred restaurants

Courchevel has seven Michelin-starred restaurants, six of them in Courchevel 1850, most within its hotels. The list below follows the **Michelin Guide France 2026**:

| Restaurant | Stars | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Le 1947 à Cheval Blanc | Three | Cheval Blanc, Courchevel 1850 |
| Sylvestre Wahid (closed, see below) | Two | Les Grandes Alpes, Courchevel 1850 |
| Slalom 1850 (formerly Baumanière 1850) | Two | Le Strato, Courchevel 1850 |
| Le Chabichou by Stéphane Buron | One | Le Chabichou, Courchevel 1850 |
| L'Altitude | One | Le K2 Altitude, Courchevel 1850 |
| Alpage | One | Annapurna, Courchevel 1850 |
| Le Farçon | One | La Tania |

A few notes on the changes for this winter. L'Altitude, led by Guillaume Duchemin and Davide Sessa, received its first star in 2026. Le Chabichou moved from two stars to one. Slalom 1850 at Le Strato, the restaurant of chef Thomas Prod'homme, is the name now used by Michelin for the table previously known as Baumanière 1850. Le Sarkara at Le K2 Palace has been reported closed for the 2026/27 winter, so it is not listed here. Sylvestre Wahid is still in the guide, but the restaurant has been closed since a fire at Les Grandes Alpes on 27 January 2026, and no reopening has been announced.

Several starred restaurants in Courchevel open for dinner only, some may close one evening a week, and their seasons can start a little after the resort opens. Many have only a handful of tables. We check opening days for your dates before we book.

## Mountain restaurants

Lunch on the slopes is part of the rhythm of a day in Courchevel. These restaurants sit on or beside the pistes:

- **Le Chalet de Pierres**, Courchevel 1850: on the Verdons slope at the Jardin Alpin, reachable on foot, on skis or by car, with traditional cooking and a sunny terrace.
- **Le Cap Horn**, Courchevel 1850: on the route de l'Altiport, open since 1954 and described by the tourist office as the oldest altitude restaurant in 1850, with an emphasis on seafood.
- **La Cave des Creux**, above the altiport: at 2,112 metres, at the top of the Gravelles area, known for Savoyard cooking.
- **La Soucoupe**, at the top of La Loze, above Courchevel 1850.
- **Le Panoramic**, at the top of La Saulire, with views across the Trois Vallées.
- **La Cendrée**, on the Courchevel 1850 ski area.
- **Le Bel Air**, 1650 Moriond: at the top of the Ariondaz gondola, with a fireplace and a south-facing terrace.
- **Le Bouc Blanc**, La Tania: at 2,000 metres at the top of the La Tania gondola, with a large terrace and Savoyard dishes.

Some mountain restaurants also open for dinner on certain evenings, with access by snowcat or car. We arrange the transfer so you do not have to think about the way back.

## Restaurants by village

Beyond the starred tables, each village has its own places for a relaxed lunch or dinner:

- **Courchevel 1850:** the hotel restaurants of Le K2 Palace (L'Altiplano), Annapurna (La Fèrma), Aman Le Mélézin (Akari) and Les Airelles (La Table des Airelles – Le Festin), as well as the INEOS Club House at the foot of the Émile-Allais stadium. In the village, Jaïs serves Lebanese cooking and Sumosan Japanese, and Le San Marco is in the heart of the resort.
- **1650 Moriond:** La Rôtisserie at the foot of the slopes, Schuss Café at the bottom of the lifts, and Copiña.
- **1550 Village:** La Cortona, just below the gondolas.
- **1300 Le Praz:** La Table de Mon Grand-Père, La Glacière and Le Kiosque du Praz.
- **La Tania:** Le Farçon, Bistrot Machet and La Ferme de La Tania on the snow front.

Opening periods change from one winter to the next, so we confirm each restaurant is open for your dates when we book.

## Children, allergies and special requests

Policies on children at dinner vary from one starred restaurant to another, long tasting menus suit adults better, and some dining rooms have very few seats. For families, a lunch on a slope-side terrace or an early dinner in a village restaurant is often more comfortable, with the starred evening kept for the adults while a nanny stays at the chalet.

Allergies and dietary requirements need notice, especially for tasting menus. When we book, we pass on every requirement in advance: allergies, vegetarian or vegan menus, religious requirements, and anything you would rather avoid. We do the same for birthdays and anniversaries, so a cake, a candle or a particular bottle is ready without you having to ask at the table.

If you are planning a larger group dinner, a private room or the private hire of a mountain restaurant for the evening may be possible on some dates; tell us the size of your group and we check what can be arranged.

## How far ahead to book

- **Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve:** book as soon as your dates are fixed. Many restaurants serve a special menu on these nights and the most popular tables are taken early.
- **The February holidays:** for starred restaurants and the busiest evenings in 1850, book the dinners that matter before you arrive.
- **January and March:** a few days ahead is usually enough, sometimes the same day.
- **Lunch on the slopes:** in the school holidays, reserve the popular terraces a few days in advance, especially for large groups.

Tell us your dates, the size of your group, any allergies and the kind of evening you have in mind, and we propose a plan for the week and make the reservations.
