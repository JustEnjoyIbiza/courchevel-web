## How we work, point by point

Here is what you can expect from us, before you even ask.

- **Replies:** we answer within 15 minutes from 8:00 to 22:00, and during the winter season we are available 24/7. You can reach us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
- **Proposals and quotes:** you receive a tailored proposal within 24 hours. We do not publish prices; every service is quoted personally, and nothing is booked without your agreement.
- **Licensed partners:** we never drive or fly clients ourselves. Road transfers are run by licensed chauffeur partners and flights by licensed operators; close protection is arranged only through CNAPS-authorised partners, as described on our [lifestyle and security page](/services/lifestyle/).
- **Chalets:** we never let property or hold rent. The chalet is contracted directly with its owner, a letting agency or the hotel, and we arrange everything around the stay; see our [chalets service](/services/chalets/).
- **Area:** Courchevel 1850, 1650 Moriond, 1550 Village, 1300 Le Praz and La Tania, and the wider Trois Vallées including Méribel and Val Thorens.
- **Booking for someone else:** personal assistants, family offices and travel agents can book on behalf of their clients.

More on how a request is handled is on our [about page](/about/).

## Why the choice matters in Courchevel

A stay in Courchevel relies on many people: the chalet owner or agency, chauffeurs and helicopter operators, instructors, chefs, nannies and sometimes security. A concierge does not replace them; it chooses them, books them and makes them work together. The quality of the stay therefore depends less on promises and more on how a concierge answers, who it works with and how clearly it explains what you are paying for. The questions below are worth asking anyone, and we are happy to answer every one of them.

## The checklist: ten questions to ask

1. **Who answers, and how quickly?** Ask for a reply time in writing and whether a person, not an automated form, handles your request. In high season, a request left unanswered for a day can mean the chalet or the table is gone.
2. **Are they in the resort during the season?** Local presence matters when a flight is delayed, a ski boot pinches or a dinner needs moving at short notice. Ask whether someone is reachable at night in the winter.
3. **How are chauffeurs licensed?** In France, private-hire drivers (VTC) need a professional card, and the operating company must be registered on the national VTC register. Ask whether transfers are run by licensed chauffeur companies, and what happens if the weather stops a helicopter.
4. **Who are the ski instructors and guides?** Teaching skiing for payment requires a state diploma, the diplôme d'État de ski – moniteur national de ski alpin. Off-piste days and ski touring call for a state-qualified high-mountain guide. Heli-skiing drops are banned in France, so any heli-skiing offer should be a day trip to Italy or Switzerland.
5. **How is security provided?** Close protection is regulated by the CNAPS, and agents need a professional card covering the protection of persons. A concierge should name the licensed company, not simply promise "security".
6. **Who signs the chalet contract?** In France, letting property on behalf of an owner is a regulated activity: an intermediary needs a professional card issued by the chamber of commerce (CCI) under the Hoguet law. Ask who the contract is with, who holds your rent and deposit, and whether the concierge is acting as a licensed agent or simply arranging the stay around a contract you sign directly.
7. **How is your data handled?** Under the GDPR you can ask any company what personal data it keeps about you and why. Ask how much of your information is shared with suppliers, and whether a first name or reference can be used instead of your full name.
8. **How are quotes presented?** A clear quote is personal to your request, shows each service separately and is approved by you before anything is booked. Be wary of vague totals or bookings made without written confirmation.
9. **Can they show references?** Reviews and references are useful when they are attributed and recent. Ask whether you can speak to a previous client or see reviews that name the service and the season.
10. **Which languages do they work in?** Make sure the person handling your stay, and the staff in the chalet, can work comfortably in your language.

## Questions about the wider area

If your plans reach beyond one village, check that the concierge covers them. The Courchevel area has five villages, from 1850 to [Courchevel 1550 Village](/courchevel-1550-village/) and [La Tania](/la-tania/), and the lifts link the whole Trois Vallées, so a lunch in Méribel or a day in Val Thorens is part of many stays. Ask also whether they plan the journey itself; our guide to [getting to Courchevel](/guides/getting-to-courchevel/) shows how much depends on the day and the route.

If you are staying in a palace hotel, its concierge desk may already cover much of what you need. Our comparison of a [private concierge and a hotel concierge](/guides/private-concierge-vs-hotel-concierge/) helps you decide.
