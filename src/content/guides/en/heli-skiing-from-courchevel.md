## Why heli-skiing is not allowed in Courchevel

Heli-skiing is not allowed anywhere in the French Alps. The Mountain Law of 1985 banned the dropping of passengers by aircraft for leisure in mountain areas, and the rule now sits in article L363-1 of the French Environment Code. Since a law of 21 February 2022, the ban covers picking passengers up as well as dropping them off, which closed the practice of collecting skiers by helicopter at the bottom of an off-piste descent. The only exception is landing on an aerodrome, which is why helicopters can still bring guests to Courchevel Altiport.

In practice, a heli-skiing trip from Courchevel is a day across the border in Italy or Switzerland. It is a long day, but for experienced off-piste skiers it is one of the most memorable things to do during a stay in the Trois Vallées.

## Heli-skiing in Italy: the Aosta Valley

The closest heli-skiing region to Courchevel is the Aosta Valley in Italy, on the far side of Mont Blanc. Local guide companies fly from areas such as La Thuile, Valgrisenche and Courmayeur, with descents on glaciers and open slopes well away from the lifts. A day usually combines one to several flights, each followed by a long guided run, and groups are small.

The road over the Petit-Saint-Bernard pass, the direct route between the Tarentaise and La Thuile, is closed in winter. The journey therefore goes round through the Mont Blanc Tunnel, which makes for an early start. We arrange the chauffeured transfer with a licensed partner and time it to the guide's meeting point.

## Heli-skiing in Switzerland: Verbier and the Valais

In Switzerland, helicopters may land for heli-skiing only at designated mountain landing sites, listed by the Federal Office of Civil Aviation. Around Verbier, the local guides fly to sites such as the Petit Combin, the Rosablanche, the Trient glacier and the Pigne d'Arolla, with long descents in high mountain terrain. The Verbier guides list their winter season from mid-December to the end of April.

Verbier is a long drive from Courchevel, so some guests add a night in Switzerland or combine the trip with an onward journey through Geneva. We plan the logistics around your stay, not the other way round.

## Guides and safety

Every heli-skiing day we arrange is led by a certified IFMGA (UIAGM) mountain guide, who chooses the slopes according to the snowpack, the weather and the avalanche bulletin. The bulletin uses a European scale of 1 to 5, and the guide may change the plan, reduce the number of flights or cancel when the risk is too high.

Before the first flight the guide checks each person's avalanche transceiver, shovel and probe and explains how to board and leave the helicopter safely. You need to be a strong, confident off-piste skier: the runs are long, the snow can change several times on one descent, and there are no pistes to fall back on. A few days off-piste with a guide in Courchevel beforehand, through our [ski and mountain service](/services/ski/), is the right way to prepare.

## When to go and what to expect

Ideal days come after fresh snow followed by clear, settled weather, when visibility is good and the snowpack has had time to stabilise. Helicopters fly by sight, so the outing is always confirmed on the morning, and a day with low cloud or strong wind is postponed rather than forced. We keep the date flexible and hold a back-up plan, such as a guided off-piste day in the Trois Vallées.

Expect an early departure, a briefing with the guide at the helicopter base, a few hours of flying and skiing, and the journey back in the late afternoon. Dress for altitude and bring a backpack for the safety equipment; the guide will tell you what else is needed.

## A scenic flight from Courchevel Altiport

For non-skiers, or on days when a trip across the border does not fit, a scenic helicopter flight from Courchevel Altiport is a good alternative. Flights last from about 15 minutes to an hour, over the Trois Vallées or towards the Mont Blanc massif, and land back at the altiport. Like every flight we arrange, they are operated by licensed aviation partners; we never fly clients ourselves.
