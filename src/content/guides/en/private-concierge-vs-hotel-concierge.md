## Three kinds of concierge in Courchevel

When people ask for a concierge in Courchevel, they may mean one of three quite different services.

- **A hotel concierge desk.** The palace and five-star hotels of Courchevel 1850 have excellent concierge desks for their guests. They know the hotel inside out, are on site day and night, and handle restaurant tables, ski lessons, spa bookings and cars with ease.
- **A chalet agency's in-house service.** Many letting agencies offer services to the guests of the chalets they let: staff, groceries, ski passes, sometimes transfers. The service is built around the property and the rental.
- **A private concierge.** An independent concierge works for you rather than for a building. It covers the whole trip, from before you leave home to the day you go back, wherever you stay and whatever you book.

None of these is better in principle. Each makes sense in a different situation.

## When a hotel concierge is enough

If you are staying in a palace hotel, most of your plans revolve around it and your group is under one roof, the hotel's own desk is usually the simplest choice. It has direct access to the hotel's restaurants, spa and drivers, and staff are on hand whenever you walk past the desk.

## When a chalet agency service fits

If you have rented a chalet through an agency and want the basics handled – staff, groceries, passes – the agency's own service is a natural first step. It knows the property, and the arrangements sit alongside your rental contract.

## When a private concierge makes more sense

- **You are staying in a chalet** and want transfers, a chef, instructors, nannies and evenings out arranged as one plan, rather than several separate bookings.
- **Your plans start before you arrive.** Choosing the village, the chalet or hotel, the flights and the helicopter or car; our guide to [getting to Courchevel](/guides/getting-to-courchevel/) shows how much the journey depends on the day.
- **You plan across several venues.** A lunch in Méribel, a dinner in 1850, a quieter base in [Courchevel 1550 Village](/courchevel-1550-village/) or [La Tania](/la-tania/).
- **Your group is split.** Family in one chalet, friends in a hotel, staff in a third place: one contact keeps everyone on the same schedule.
- **Requests fall between the big bookings.** Gifts, personal shopping, errands or close protection through licensed partners, as on our [lifestyle page](/services/lifestyle/).

## How the three can work together

The three can be combined. A family might stay in a palace hotel while grandparents rent a chalet nearby; the hotel desk looks after the hotel, the agency looks after the chalet, and a private concierge arranges the transfers, the shared dinners and the childcare so that both sides stay in step. We contact the hotel or the agency directly where needed, so arrangements are not duplicated.
