## Your chalet, looked after all year

A chalet in the mountains needs attention even when nobody is staying. Snow, frost and long empty weeks can turn a small issue into a major repair. We act as your eyes and hands in Courchevel:

- **Key holding:** we keep a set of keys and let in contractors, cleaners and deliveries.
- **Property checks:** regular visits at a frequency you choose, with a report and photographs.
- **After storms and heavy snowfall:** an extra visit to check roofs, access and heating.
- **Emergencies:** someone on site quickly to deal with a leak, a power cut or an alarm.

## Welcoming you and your guests

Whether you arrive yourself or lend the chalet to family and friends, we prepare it before arrival. The heating is on, the beds are made, the fridge is stocked to your list and fresh flowers are on the table. On arrival we meet your guests, hand over the keys and show them how the chalet works, from the spa to the ski room.

During the stay, your guests can call on us for anything else they need: a private chef, ski lessons, transfers or a nanny.

## Cleaning and maintenance

We coordinate the trades that keep a chalet in good condition:

- **Cleaning** between stays, deep cleans at the start and end of the season, and linen.
- **Technical systems:** heating, hot water, pool and spa, alarms and home automation.
- **Outside:** snow clearing, roof checks, terraces and garden in summer.
- **Repairs and works:** quotes from local tradespeople, supervision and follow-up.

You approve each piece of work before it starts, and we send photographs when it is done.

## Working with your rental agency

If you let your chalet, letting itself is handled by your rental agency or manager. In France, letting a property for others, collecting rent or deposits and signing rental contracts are regulated activities that require a professional licence, and we do not offer them.

What we do is support the practical side of each stay: preparing the chalet, welcoming guests, handling cleaning and maintenance, and reporting back to you and your agency. That way the guest experience and the condition of your chalet stay consistent. Your agency keeps the contract and the relationship with the tenant; we keep the chalet ready, clean and in working order between stays.

## Year-round contact

Owners abroad often want one local contact rather than a list of tradespeople. We keep a record of your chalet's systems, suppliers and preferences, so every visit, repair and arrival follows the same standard.

Between seasons we stay in touch with regular reports and remain your contact in Courchevel. During the winter season we are available 24/7, and messages sent between 8:00 and 22:00 are answered within 15 minutes.
