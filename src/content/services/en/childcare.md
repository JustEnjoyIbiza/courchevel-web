## A nanny who fits your family

The right nanny depends on your children and your days in the mountains. Before we propose anyone, we ask:

- **Ages and routines:** naps, meals, allergies and bedtimes.
- **Hours:** full days while you ski, mornings only, or evenings after dinner.
- **Languages:** the language your children speak at home and any second language you would like them to hear.
- **Ski days:** whether the nanny will walk the children to ski school, wait during the lesson and bring them back.

We check every nanny's identity, references and experience with young children. In France, the recognised entry-level childcare qualification is a state diploma, the CAP Accompagnant éducatif petite enfance. Where a nanny holds it, or a higher nursing or early-years qualification, we tell you. You meet or speak with the nanny before the first day whenever you wish.

## Evening babysitting

Many families want the evenings to themselves: dinner in Courchevel 1850, a table at an altitude restaurant or a quiet night in the chalet's spa. We arrange a babysitter who arrives before you leave, follows your bedtime routine and stays until you return. For a series of evenings we try to keep the same person, so the children see a familiar face.

## Ski lessons and activities for children

Courchevel is a good place to learn. Ski schools in each village run fenced snow gardens for the youngest skiers, and group lessons usually start at 3 years old. In Courchevel 1850, the Village des Enfants combines a day nursery for children from 18 months with a ski club for ages 3 to 12.

We book the format that suits each child:

- **Group lessons** for children who enjoy company and a steady programme.
- **Private lessons** for shy beginners, mixed-age siblings or children who want to move faster.
- **Activities off the slopes:** ice skating, the Aquamotion aquatic centre, snowshoe walks, sledging and craft afternoons in the chalet.

In the Christmas, February and Easter holidays, lessons fill early. Send us the children's ages and levels as soon as your dates are fixed.

## Baby equipment, ready on arrival

Travelling with a baby means a lot of luggage. We arrange cots, high chairs, baby baths, buggies suited to snow, stair gates and car seats for your transfer. Everything is delivered and set up before you land, and collected after you leave.

## During your stay

We are on call throughout, 24/7 in season. If a child is unwell and you need a doctor, if you want an extra evening of babysitting, or if a lesson needs to move, one message is enough. We cover Courchevel 1850, 1650 Moriond, 1550 Village, 1300 Le Praz and La Tania, as well as Méribel and Val Thorens.
