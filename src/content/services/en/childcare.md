## How we choose a nanny

We check every nanny's identity, references and experience with young children, and confirm the languages each one speaks before the booking. In France, the recognised entry-level childcare qualification is a state diploma, the CAP Accompagnant éducatif petite enfance; where a nanny holds it, or a higher nursing or early-years qualification, we tell you. On ski days we agree whether the nanny walks the children to ski school, waits during the lesson and brings them back. For a series of evenings we try to keep the same babysitter, so the children see a familiar face.

## Courchevel for children

Ski schools in each village run fenced snow gardens for the youngest skiers, and group lessons usually start at 3 years old. In Courchevel 1850, the Village des Enfants combines a day nursery for children from 18 months with a ski club for ages 3 to 12, and places are limited in the school holidays. Our guide to [Courchevel with kids](/guides/courchevel-with-kids/) covers ski school ages, activities and which village suits your family.
