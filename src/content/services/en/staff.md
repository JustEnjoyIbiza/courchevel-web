## The household team

The right team depends on the size of the chalet and how you like to live. A couple in a three-bedroom chalet may want discreet daily housekeeping; a family of twelve may prefer a full team with a manager. We propose the staff that fit your stay, and adjust the team if your group grows or changes during the week:

- **Butler or majordomo:** your first point of contact in the house, from breakfast service to evening drinks.
- **Chalet manager:** runs the property and the team, handles suppliers and keeps the house to your standards.
- **Housekeepers:** daily cleaning, bed linen, towels and turndown in the evening.
- **Head waiter and serving staff:** table service for dinners, lunches and parties.

## Laundry and garment care

Ski clothes, evening wear and children's outfits all need attention in the mountains. Snow, salt and wet boots take their toll, and a week of skiing produces more laundry than most guests expect. Housekeeping staff take care of washing, ironing and pressing, and delicate pieces go to a specialist cleaner. In the ski room, gloves, boots and base layers are dried and ready for the next morning.

At the end of the stay, staff pack your cases, separate what needs cleaning and arrange for anything left behind to be sent on.

## Staff for owners and for single events

If you own a chalet in Courchevel, we can staff it for your holiday weeks or the whole season, and brief the team on the house and your routines before you arrive. For a dinner or celebration in a rented chalet, we add serving staff and a head waiter for a single evening, working alongside the chef.

## How we work

Before each stay we brief the staff on your guests, children, allergies, preferred routines and privacy. Discretion is part of the role: staff are briefed not to discuss guests or share photographs of the chalet, and they keep to the hours and spaces you choose.

A typical day for a fully staffed chalet might look like this:

- **Morning:** breakfast served, ski clothes laid out, boots warmed in the ski room.
- **Daytime:** rooms cleaned, laundry done, fresh flowers and supplies checked.
- **Evening:** drinks and dinner served, beds turned down, the house prepared for the next day.

During the stay you have one contact for every change, and we remain available 24/7 during the winter season.

Send us your dates, the chalet and the number of guests. We reply within 15 minutes between 8:00 and 22:00. Within 24 hours you receive a staffing proposal setting out each role, the working hours and how the team fits around your days on the slopes.
