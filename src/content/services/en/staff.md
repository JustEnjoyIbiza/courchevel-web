## Discretion

Before each stay we brief the staff on your guests, children, allergies, preferred routines and privacy. Discretion is part of the role: staff are briefed not to discuss guests or share photographs of the chalet, and they keep to the hours and spaces you choose.

## A day in a fully staffed chalet

In the morning, breakfast is served, ski clothes are laid out and boots are warmed in the ski room. During the day, rooms are cleaned, laundry is done and flowers and supplies are checked. In the evening, drinks and dinner are served, beds are turned down and the house is prepared for the next day. A couple in a three-bedroom chalet may want discreet daily housekeeping; a family of twelve may prefer a full team with a manager. We adjust the team if your group grows or changes during the week, with one contact for every change of plan and 24/7 availability during the winter season.
