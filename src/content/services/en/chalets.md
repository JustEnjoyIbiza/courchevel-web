## Choosing the right chalet

The right chalet depends on more than the number of bedrooms. Before we send a shortlist we ask about the things that change daily life in the mountains:

- **Location:** ski-in, ski-out access, or a short walk to the centre and the restaurants.
- **Group:** ages of the children, guests who do not ski, and anyone who needs a bedroom on the ground floor.
- **Service:** a fully staffed chalet with chef and butler, or more privacy with daily housekeeping only.
- **Wellness:** pool, spa, hammam, gym or a cinema room for bad-weather days.

We then propose a small number of chalets that genuinely fit, rather than a long list.

## Palace and five-star hotels

Courchevel 1850 is known for having several of France's palace-rated hotels within walking distance of each other. A hotel can make more sense than a chalet for shorter stays, couples, or groups who want a spa, restaurants and room service without staff to manage. We arrange the booking and look after everything around it: transfers, ski passes, restaurant tables and in-room requests.

## Before you arrive

Most of the work happens before you land. We confirm the arrival time with the transfer, brief the chalet staff on allergies and preferences, stock the kitchen and cellar, and prepare the rooms for children. For Christmas and New Year we set up the tree and the decoration, so the holiday starts the moment you arrive.

## How the booking works

We search, shortlist and arrange; the chalet itself is rented from its owner or letting agency, or booked with the hotel. You receive the rental or hotel terms from them, and we coordinate everything around the stay: arrival times, staff, transfers and requests. This keeps the contract simple and clear, and lets us focus on the parts of the stay that make the difference.

## What to send us

The fastest way to a good shortlist is a short message with:

- your dates, and whether they are flexible by a day or two;
- the number of adults and children, and the children's ages;
- the village you prefer, or what matters most if you are unsure;
- the services you want in the chalet: chef, butler, nanny, driver.

## During your stay

We stay on call for the whole stay, 24/7 in season. If you want to change a dinner, add a guest, extend by a night or move a ski lesson, one message is enough.
