## Après-ski on the slopes and in the village

Après-ski in Courchevel begins on the mountain. As the lifts close in the late afternoon, terraces and slope-side bars fill with music, and the evening then moves down into the village. We request tables for your group, so you arrive straight from the last run without queuing for a place.

If some of your group do not ski, we time the table so they can meet you there, and arrange a car to bring them up. For a quieter evening, we suggest a fireside bar in the village or a drink on a heated terrace instead of the busiest spots.

## VIP nightclub tables and guest lists

Courchevel 1850 has the liveliest nights in the resort, with restaurants, bars and nightclubs close together. Over Christmas, New Year's Eve and the February holidays the most popular tables are in high demand.

- **Tables:** we request VIP tables on your behalf, with the number of guests and your preferred time. Each venue confirms subject to availability.
- **Guest lists:** we submit names for you and your friends where the venue offers a list.
- **Planning:** dinner booked beforehand, a driver waiting afterwards, and any special bottles or celebrations passed on in advance.

## Private parties in your chalet

Some of the liveliest nights in Courchevel happen at home. For a birthday, a New Year's Eve dinner or simply an evening with friends, we organise a party in your chalet:

- DJ or live musicians, and sound and lighting set up in the living room.
- Bartenders and serving staff, with cocktails and champagne.
- Catering by a private chef, from canapés to a seated dinner.
- Decoration, then the clean-up the next morning.

We check the chalet's house rules on noise and guest numbers with the owner beforehand.

## Planning a night out

A good evening in Courchevel usually follows the same rhythm: après-ski on the slopes, a short rest at the chalet, a late dinner in the village, then the club. We plan each step so the timings fit together:

- **Dinner:** a table booked at an hour that leaves time to get ready after skiing.
- **Transport:** a driver for the whole evening, with room for coats and any guests who join later.
- **Children:** an evening babysitter at the chalet, so parents can go out without watching the clock.
- **The next morning:** a later start, breakfast at the chalet and ski lessons moved if needed.

## How to book

Tell us your dates, the size of your group and the kind of evening you have in mind. We answer within 15 minutes between 8:00 and 22:00 and send a tailored proposal within 24 hours. In the winter season we are on call 24/7, late nights included, if you want to add a table or change plans.
