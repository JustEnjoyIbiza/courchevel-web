## Where the evening goes

Après-ski in Courchevel begins on the mountain as the lifts close in the late afternoon, then moves down into the village. Courchevel 1850 has the liveliest nights, with restaurants, bars and nightclubs within walking distance of each other. The evening venues our guests ask for most each winter:

- **Bagatelle**, on the slopes of Courchevel 1850, for a long, festive lunch that runs into the afternoon.
- **Le Tremplin**, at the foot of the slopes in 1850, for après-ski as the lifts close.
- **La Mangeoire**, in the village, a piano bar and restaurant where dinner turns into the evening.

Each venue confirms subject to availability. For lunch and dinner addresses, see our guide to [where to eat in Courchevel](/guides/where-to-eat/).

## A quieter night, or a later one

If some of your group do not ski, we time the table so they can meet you there and send a car to bring them up. For a quieter evening, we suggest a fireside bar in the village or a drink on a heated terrace instead of the busiest spots. In the winter season we are on call 24/7, late nights included, to add a table or change plans.
