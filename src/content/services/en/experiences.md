## Fitted around your ski days

Most activities take a few hours at most, so they fit easily into a ski day: a morning on the pistes, lunch, then an activity before the light fades. We book each one with qualified local providers, choose the time of day that suits it and arrange the transfer from your chalet. Warm clothing and any equipment are arranged in advance, and we check each provider's minimum age before booking. Our [example week in Courchevel 1850](/guides/a-week-in-courchevel-1850/) shows how activities fit around ski days over a full stay, and our [signature experiences](/experiences/) combine several of them into one day, from a private ski day to a dinner above the valley.

## Ice driving in Val Thorens

The ice circuit is in [Val Thorens](/val-thorens/), the highest resort in the Trois Vallées, which also makes a fine ski day from Courchevel. A session with an instructor teaches car control on ice and snow; we book it, arrange a chauffeur for the return and fit it around your ski days.
