## On the snow, away from the pistes

Some of the most memorable afternoons in Courchevel happen off skis. We book each activity with qualified local providers, choose the time of day that suits it and arrange the transfer from your chalet.

- **Snowmobiling:** first rides on a secure 700 m circuit, or a guided outing on the ski area once the slopes have closed and the light is fading.
- **Dog sledding:** sit back as a passenger, or learn to drive your own team with a musher beside you. Rides follow forest trails and take place by day or at dusk.
- **Ski-joëring:** skiing while towed by a horse, a traditional winter discipline that suits confident skiers.
- **Snowshoe walks:** quiet guided walks through the forest, with stops for wildlife, views or a hot drink.

## In the air

Courchevel Altiport sits just above Courchevel 1850, which makes it an easy starting point for a flight.

- **Tandem paragliding:** a flight with a state-certified pilot, taking off above the resort and landing lower down the valley. Flights depend on the wind, so we hold a second slot when we can.
- **Scenic helicopter flights:** a loop over the Trois Vallées, or a longer flight towards the Mont Blanc massif, run by licensed helicopter partners.

## Ice driving

For those who enjoy driving, the ice circuit at Val Thorens is around 800 m long at about 2,200 m altitude. A session with an instructor teaches car control on ice and snow. We book the session, arrange a chauffeur for the return and fit it around your ski days.

The circuit is in [Val Thorens](/val-thorens/), the highest resort in the Trois Vallées, which also makes a fine ski day from Courchevel.

## For families

Children often remember the evenings more than the pistes, and many of these activities suit a short winter afternoon after ski school. A floodlit sledging run links Courchevel 1850 to Courchevel 1550 Village, and the ice rink at the Forum in Courchevel 1850 is open between 4 December 2026 and 18 April 2027. Dog sledding as passengers and short snowshoe walks also work well for younger children, and we check each provider's minimum age before booking.

## How to plan

Tell us who is coming, their ages and what appeals. Our first reply comes within 15 minutes (8:00 to 22:00) and a tailored programme within 24 hours. In season we are on call 24/7, so a cancelled flight can become a dog-sledding afternoon the same day.

Most activities take a few hours at most, so they fit easily into a ski day: a morning on the pistes, lunch, then an activity before the light fades. Warm clothing, transfers and any equipment are arranged in advance, and we check the weather forecast the evening before so plans can change before anyone sets out.
