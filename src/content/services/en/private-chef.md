## A private chef in your chalet

A chef who cooks in your chalet changes the rhythm of a ski holiday: no reservations to keep, children eat when they are hungry, and the evening ends by the fire. We propose chefs matched to your group and your taste, for a single dinner, every evening or the whole stay.

Before the first meal the chef receives your brief:

- **Guests:** number of adults and children, and who joins which meals.
- **Diet:** allergies, intolerances, religious requirements and dislikes.
- **Style:** Savoyard classics, light Mediterranean, Japanese, or a tasting menu for a special evening.

Michelin-starred chefs can be requested for a birthday, an anniversary or New Year's Eve, subject to availability.

The chef shops locally each day, so the menu follows what is fresh and seasonal in Savoie, from Beaufort and Reblochon to lake fish and mountain charcuterie. If a guest wants something specific, such as a favourite dish from home or caviar for an evening, we source it in advance. Children's meals can be served earlier, separately from the adults' dinner, and the chef clears the kitchen before leaving.

## Breakfast, wine and a stocked kitchen

The details around the meals matter as much as the dinner itself. We arrange:

- **Breakfast in the chalet:** fresh bread, pastries, fruit and eggs prepared each morning before skiing.
- **A sommelier:** for a dinner, a tasting or advice on building your cellar for the week.
- **Wine and champagne:** delivered to the chalet and topped up during your stay.
- **Grocery stocking:** the fridge, pantry and bar filled to your list before you arrive, including baby food and children's favourites.

## Restaurant reservations

Courchevel has a remarkable concentration of restaurants, from sunny mountain terraces to gastronomic dining rooms in Courchevel 1850. In the festive weeks and February the most sought-after tables fill quickly, so we request reservations on your behalf as early as possible, subject to availability. We can also arrange lunch on the slopes timed around your ski lesson, and a driver for the evening.

If you would like to try restaurants elsewhere in the Trois Vallées, we book tables in Méribel or Val Thorens and arrange the transfer there and back, so nobody needs to drive after dinner.

## How to book

To see how chef dinners and restaurant evenings can alternate over a stay, read our [example week in Courchevel 1850](/guides/a-week-in-courchevel-1850/).

Send us your dates, the number of guests and how many meals you would like in the chalet. If you are not sure yet, we can start with a few dinners and add more once you have settled in. You hear from us within 15 minutes between 8:00 and 22:00, with a tailored proposal and sample menus within 24 hours. In season we are on call 24/7 to add a dinner, a guest or a bottle.
