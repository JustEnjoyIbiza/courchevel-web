## Celebrations in the mountains

A celebration in Courchevel can be as small as a dinner for two or as large as a party for the whole chalet and friends from other villages. Before we propose anything we ask:

- **The occasion:** birthday, anniversary, proposal, wedding, New Year's Eve or a company event.
- **The guests:** number, ages, and anyone who does not ski or needs easy access.
- **The setting:** your chalet, a private room in a restaurant, or a terrace on the mountain.
- **The mood:** a long dinner, a party with a DJ, or something quiet and personal.

We then coordinate every supplier and run the timetable on the day, so you can be a guest at your own event. For larger gatherings we also arrange transport between villages, cloakroom and serving staff, and a quiet room for children if families are invited.

## Proposals and weddings

For a proposal, discretion matters most. We arrange the setting, the flowers and, if you wish, a photographer positioned out of sight. A private dinner in the chalet is often the simplest choice; a terrace at sunset or a viewpoint reached on skis works for couples who prefer the outdoors.

For weddings, it helps to know the French rules early. A civil ceremony takes place at the town hall of a commune where one partner, or a parent, is domiciled or has lived for at least a month, after banns have been published. Most couples from abroad therefore marry at home and hold a symbolic ceremony and celebration in Courchevel. We plan the setting, officiant, music, flowers, dinner and accommodation for your guests.

## New Year's Eve

The resort traditionally marks midnight with fireworks, and 31 December is the busiest night of the season. Private chefs, DJs, musicians and serving staff are reserved months ahead. If you are planning a dinner or party in the chalet, send us your request as soon as your dates are confirmed. We also arrange tables for New Year's Eve dinners in restaurants and hotels, and transport for the evening.

For the whole festive fortnight, from the chalet to the last transfer, see our page on [Christmas and New Year’s Eve in Courchevel](/christmas-new-year-courchevel/).

## Corporate events and incentive groups

Courchevel works well for leadership retreats, client hospitality and incentive trips. We plan:

- **Accommodation** across chalets and hotels for the whole group.
- **Transfers** from Geneva, Lyon or Chambéry, and helicopter arrivals for senior guests.
- **Programmes** of ski lessons, mountain activities and team dinners.
- **Meeting spaces** in a chalet or hotel, with the equipment you need.

How a team stay comes together, from rooms to the closing dinner, is set out on our page on [corporate retreats in Courchevel](/corporate-retreats-courchevel/).

## The suppliers behind the evening

Each event draws on a small team: florists, decorators, private chefs and sommeliers, DJs and live musicians, photographers and videographers, hairdressers and make-up artists. We brief each of them, confirm timings and stay on call during the event, 24/7 in season, so last-minute changes are handled quietly.
