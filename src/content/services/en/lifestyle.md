## Shopping, gifts and errands

Courchevel 1850 has many fashion, jewellery and ski-wear boutiques within a short walk of each other. Tell us your sizes, the colours and brands you like and the occasions ahead, and we prepare a selection before your first appointment, so the time you spend shopping is short and focused. Styling for a gala evening or New Year's Eve can include alterations and delivery before the evening. For gifts, send us a list: we buy, wrap and deliver them to your chalet or to friends staying elsewhere in the Trois Vallées. Tickets for concerts, sporting events and shows are bought through official channels only.

## Close protection and residential security

In France, private security is regulated by the CNAPS (Conseil national des activités privées de sécurité), and close-protection agents must hold a professional card that covers the protection of persons. We arrange close protection for individuals or families, residential security for the chalet day and night, and secure transfers coordinated with your drivers, only through licensed, CNAPS-authorised partners. Each request is discussed in confidence, and planning starts with your schedule, the chalet's layout and the level of visibility you are comfortable with.
