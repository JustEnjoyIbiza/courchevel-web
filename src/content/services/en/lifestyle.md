## Personal shopping and styling

Courchevel 1850 has many fashion, jewellery and ski-wear boutiques within a short walk of each other. A personal shopper saves you the time between them. We arrange:

- **Private appointments** in boutiques, when the house allows it.
- **Selections brought to your chalet** to try in comfort.
- **Styling** for a gala evening, a New Year's Eve dinner or your ski wardrobe.
- **Alterations** and delivery before the evening.

Tell us your sizes, the colours and brands you like and the occasions ahead, and we prepare a selection before your first appointment, so the time you spend shopping is short and focused.

## Watches, jewellery and gifts

For a significant piece we can arrange viewings, check availability across boutiques and organise secure delivery. For gifts, send us a list, and we buy, wrap and deliver them to your chalet or to friends staying elsewhere in the Trois Vallées. At Christmas we can collect and wrap presents for the whole family, ready under the tree.

## Errands, tickets and pet care

Much of a good stay is in the small things. We handle:

- **Errands:** dry cleaning, pharmacy, newspapers, flowers, a forgotten charger or adaptor.
- **Tickets:** concerts, sporting events and shows in the Alps, Paris or abroad, through official channels.
- **Pet care:** walks, sitting while you ski or dine, grooming and a vet appointment if needed.
- **Last-minute requests:** a cake for tonight, a dinner jacket, a car at short notice.

## Close protection and residential security

Some guests travel with their own security; others need it locally. In France, private security is regulated by the CNAPS (Conseil national des activités privées de sécurité), and close-protection agents must hold a professional card that covers the protection of persons.

We arrange close protection and residential security only through licensed, CNAPS-authorised security partners. Depending on your needs this can include:

- **Close protection** for individuals or families during the stay.
- **Residential security** for the chalet, day and night.
- **Secure transfers** coordinated with your drivers and arrival times.

We discuss each request in confidence, and planning starts with your schedule, the chalet's layout and the level of visibility you are comfortable with.

## One message is enough

Many of these requests arrive late in the evening or on the day itself, and that is expected. Send a message by WhatsApp, phone or email with what you need and where you are, and we tell you what is possible and how quickly. From 8:00 to 22:00 we answer within 15 minutes, and in the winter season we are on call 24/7. We cover Courchevel 1850, 1650 Moriond, 1550 Village, 1300 Le Praz and La Tania, as well as Méribel and Val Thorens.
