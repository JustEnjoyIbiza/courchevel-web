## Massage and recovery in your chalet

After a day on the Trois Vallées pistes, the simplest luxury is not having to go out again. We arrange a massage therapist who arrives with a table, oils and fresh linen, and sets up in your chalet's spa or a quiet room. You choose the style and the pressure:

- **Relaxing massage** after travel or a long day.
- **Deep-tissue and sports recovery** for legs and back after several days of skiing.
- **Couples' treatments** with two therapists at the same time.
- **Treatments for the whole group,** one after another through the afternoon.

Tell us about injuries, pregnancy or any medical conditions in advance, so we match you with the right practitioner. For a group, we can bring two or three therapists at once, so nobody waits until late evening for their turn. If you plan to ski the next morning, a lighter recovery massage the evening before is often the better choice.

## Spa bookings in Courchevel

Courchevel 1850's palace and five-star hotels have large spas with pools, hammams and treatment rooms, and Aquamotion, the resort's aquatic centre, adds pools, saunas and a steam room. If you prefer to go out, we book treatments, hammam time or a day package, and arrange transfers so you can arrive straight from the slopes. In the festive weeks and February, treatment times go quickly; send your preferred days as early as you can.

## Personal trainers, yoga and Pilates

Some guests want to prepare their legs before the first ski day; others want to keep their routine. We arrange:

- **Personal training** in the chalet gym or outdoors, from strength work to stretching.
- **Private yoga and Pilates** in the morning before the lifts open, or at sunset.
- **Mobility and recovery sessions** for skiers with tired knees and backs.

In France, anyone paid to coach sport must hold a recognised qualification and a professional card. We only work with trainers and teachers who meet this rule.

## Hair, make-up and manicure

For a dinner at an altitude restaurant, a gala evening or New Year's Eve, we arrange hairdressers, make-up artists and manicurists who come to you. For a group we build a timetable so everyone is ready at the same time, without queuing for a mirror. Blow-dries after a day in a ski helmet are among the most frequent requests.

## How we plan it

We schedule treatments around your ski lessons, meals and transfers, and keep a note of your preferences for the rest of the stay. If you want to move a session, add a guest or book the same therapist again, one message is enough. Messages sent between 8:00 and 22:00 are answered within 15 minutes, and in the winter season we are available 24/7.
