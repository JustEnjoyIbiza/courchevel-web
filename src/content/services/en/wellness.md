## Massage and recovery in your chalet

After a day on the Trois Vallées pistes, the simplest luxury is not having to go out again. The therapist sets up in your chalet's spa or a quiet room, and you choose the style and the pressure. Tell us about injuries, pregnancy or any medical conditions in advance, so we match you with the right practitioner. If you plan to ski the next morning, a lighter recovery massage the evening before is often the better choice.

## Spas, trainers and beauty in Courchevel

Courchevel 1850's [palace and five-star hotels](/palace-hotels-courchevel/) have large spas with pools, hammams and treatment rooms, and many accept outside guests, subject to availability and the hotel's policy. Aquamotion, the resort's aquatic centre, adds pools, saunas and a steam room. In France, anyone paid to coach sport must hold a recognised qualification and a professional card; we only work with trainers and teachers who meet this rule. Hairdressers and make-up artists come to the chalet, and blow-dries after a day in a ski helmet are among the most frequent requests.
