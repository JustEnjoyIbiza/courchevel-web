## Who it suits

La Tania was built for the 1992 Albertville Winter Olympics, to house competitors, and it has kept a modest scale. Its wooden buildings sit in a clearing in the forest and were designed to blend into the landscape. The atmosphere is peaceful, and it still feels like a village rather than a resort.

It suits families with young children, skiers who want to explore both Courchevel and Méribel from the same base, and anyone who prefers calm evenings in the forest to busier resort centres. When you want a livelier night, 1850 and Méribel are both within reach by car.

## Getting there

**By road.** La Tania is about 25 minutes by road from Moûtiers, the nearest railway station and the gateway from the main airports. Chambéry is the closest airport; Geneva and Lyon Saint-Exupéry are further away. As a guide, Courchevel is about 110 km from Chambéry (1 h 20), 150 km from Geneva (2 h 15) and 187 km from Lyon (2 h to 2 h 15), and La Tania is reached along the same valley road. Snow and Saturday traffic add time, and we plan the journey around them.

**By air.** Courchevel Altiport, above 1850, takes helicopters and small aircraft in daylight and suitable weather. From there a car brings you to La Tania.

**Between villages.** On the slopes, La Tania's lifts connect with both the Courchevel and Méribel ski areas. By road, Le Praz is the nearest Courchevel village, with its gondola up to 1850. We can arrange a driver for evenings in 1850 or Méribel.

## Skiing from the village

A gondola leaves from the village and climbs into the ski area. From there, high-speed chairlifts give access to the Courchevel side, towards the slopes above 1850, and to the Méribel side via the Col de la Loze area. Together they put you on the Trois Vallées network, with around 600 km of pistes.

At the end of the day, green, blue and red pistes run down through the forest to the village. The north-east-facing slopes around La Tania often keep good snow, and the trees help visibility on grey days. We book ski school or private instructors and have equipment fitted at the chalet before the first morning.

Because La Tania sits between two ski areas, it pays to plan the route home. If you ski over to Méribel, leave enough time to catch the lifts back before they close; missing the last connection means a much longer journey by road. We can suggest itineraries for each day and keep a driver on standby in case the weather changes your plans.

## Where to eat

La Tania is small, but it has a Michelin-starred restaurant: Le Farçon, where chef Julien Machet cooks around Savoyard produce, and which you can reach on skis. Tables are limited, so we reserve as early as possible.

For other evenings, the restaurants of Le Praz are a short drive away, and the Michelin-starred tables of 1850 and the restaurants of Méribel are within reach by car. Many families also enjoy dinners at the chalet with a [private chef](/services/private-chef/).
