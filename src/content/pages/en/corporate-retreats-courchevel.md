## A retreat in the mountains, planned around your programme

A corporate retreat in Courchevel usually mixes three things: time to work together, time on the mountain and evenings that bring the group together. We start from your programme rather than from a package. Tell us who is coming, how many working sessions you need and what the trip should achieve, whether that is a strategy offsite for a leadership team, an incentive trip for a sales team or a seminar with a client.

We then propose accommodation, a meeting set-up, transfers, activities and dinners that fit, with each service quoted personally. For activities to add to the programme, from dog sledding to a dinner above the valley, see our [experiences page](/experiences/).

## Chalets, hotels and meeting space

For a leadership team of a few people, a large chalet often works well: a living room or dining room becomes the meeting space, a private chef serves lunch between sessions, and the group stays together in the evening. For larger groups we look at several chalets close to one another, or a hotel with rooms for the whole group and a space to meet.

Most groups stay in [Courchevel 1850](/courchevel-1850/), close to the lifts and restaurants. Teams that prefer a calmer base sometimes choose [Courchevel 1550 Village](/courchevel-1550-village/) or [La Tania](/la-tania/), both connected to the ski area. We check practical details before you commit: screens, connectivity, quiet rooms for calls, and the time it takes to walk or drive between the accommodation and the evening venues.

We are not a letting agency and do not hold rent. The chalet owner, agency or hotel remains your contractual partner for the accommodation.

## Group transfers and arrival day

Arrival day sets the tone. Groups often fly into Geneva, Lyon or Chambéry at different times, so we plan transfers in waves, with minibuses or several chauffeured cars, and helicopter transfers to Courchevel Altiport for those who need to arrive quickly. All transfers are run by licensed chauffeur and aviation partners; we never drive or fly clients ourselves. See our [private transfers](/services/transfers/) and the [guide to getting to Courchevel](/guides/getting-to-courchevel/) for distances and travel times.

Saturdays in high season bring the heaviest traffic in the valley, as most chalets change guests that day. A Sunday-to-Thursday or Thursday-to-Sunday retreat avoids it.

## Activities and evenings

Ski days work best with instructors grouped by level, so beginners and strong skiers each get a good day. For non-skiers, and for a change of pace, we book activities with qualified local providers: snowmobiling after the lifts close, dog sledding, snowshoe walks, tandem paragliding or a scenic helicopter flight from the altiport.

Evenings can be a private dinner in the chalet, a table for the group in a restaurant, or one larger event with music, decoration and a photographer. Our [events service](/services/events/) covers the venue, catering and entertainment for that evening.

## Logistics for assistants and event planners

Corporate stays involve many names, changing flights and late additions. We work directly with the executive assistant, office manager or event agency preparing the trip, and keep one shared, written programme up to date. For each change we confirm in writing what has moved and who has been told.

During the stay we remain on call 24/7 in the winter season for new requests, a forgotten item or a dinner that needs to move. For individual requests from executives during the trip, such as a gift, a reservation or a delivery, our [lifestyle service](/services/lifestyle/) handles them in the same thread. If you organise trips for senior staff or principals regularly, see [how we work with assistants and family offices](/assistants-and-family-offices/).

## Invoicing and paperwork

Each service is invoiced by the supplier who provides it: the chalet or hotel, the transfer company, the ski school, the restaurant or the activity provider. Tell us your requirements for invoicing, such as the company name, purchase order numbers or a cost centre reference, and we pass them to every supplier so the paperwork matches your internal process.

## Starting a request

Send us the dates, the approximate number of guests, the preferred airport and the mix of work and leisure you have in mind. We reply within 15 minutes between 8:00 and 22:00, and send a tailored proposal within 24 hours. You can read more [about how we work](/about/) before getting in touch.
