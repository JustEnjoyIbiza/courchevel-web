## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. Browsers can also keep small notes for a site in what is called local or session storage. French and European rules treat both the same way: anything that is not strictly needed for the site to work, or that you have not asked for, needs your consent first.

## What this site uses

| What | Why | How long | Consent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your cookie choice, kept in your browser's local storage | To remember whether you accepted or declined audience measurement | Until you change it or clear your browser | Not needed: it records your choice |
| Where your visit came from, kept in your browser's session storage | If you send a request, to tell us which page or campaign brought you | Until you close the tab; nothing is sent unless you send a form | Not needed: it stays in your browser |
| Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier) | To count visits and see which pages are useful, with your IP address shortened | Thirteen months at most | Needed: set only after you accept |
| Spam protection on our forms (Cloudflare Turnstile), when it is active | To check that a form is sent by a person, not a robot | During the check | Not needed: used only for security |

We do not use advertising or social media cookies, and we do not embed videos, maps or social media feeds that would set their own.

## Accepting, refusing or changing your choice

When audience measurement is active, a banner asks for your choice on your first visit. Nothing is measured until you accept, and declining takes one click, like accepting. To change your choice later, use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser's settings.
