## The festive fortnight in 2026/27

The ski area opens on **4 December 2026**, and the French Christmas holidays run from **19 December 2026 to 4 January 2027** for all zones. These two weeks are the most sought-after of the winter. Christmas Eve falls on a Thursday and New Year's Eve on a Thursday too, while most chalets change guests on the Saturdays in between.

| Date | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 4 to 18 December 2026 | Opening weeks, quieter slopes and easier bookings |
| Saturday 19 December | Holidays begin, first busy arrival day |
| Tuesday 22 December | Women's Alpine Ski World Cup, night slalom at the Émile-Allais stadium in 1850 |
| 24 and 25 December | Christmas Eve and Christmas Day |
| Saturday 26 December | Changeover day for many chalets |
| Thursday 31 December | New Year's Eve, the busiest night of the season |
| Saturday 2 January 2027 | Changeover day, many departures |
| Monday 4 January | French schools go back; the resort becomes calmer |
| Thursday 7 January | Orthodox Christmas |

Over the holidays the resort holds festive walks, parades and torchlight descents in the villages, and midnight on 31 December is traditionally marked with fireworks. For the full winter calendar, see our guide to [season dates and events](/guides/season-dates-and-events/).

## What we arrange for Christmas and New Year

Most of the work happens before you land, so the holiday starts when you walk through the door.

- **The chalet, ready on arrival.** The tree is up and decorated, the fridge and cellar are stocked to your list, and the rooms are prepared for children. If you have not yet chosen a chalet or a hotel, see our [chalets and palace hotels](/services/chalets/).
- **Festive meals at home.** A private chef plans the Christmas Eve dinner and the Christmas Day lunch with you, from a traditional menu to the dishes your family serves at home, and allergies and religious requirements are passed on in advance. See our [private chef service](/services/private-chef/).
- **New Year's Eve.** We request restaurant, hotel and club tables on your behalf, subject to availability, and arrange a driver for the evening. For a dinner or party in the chalet, we bring together the chef, serving staff and music; see our [events service](/services/events/).
- **Evenings for the adults.** A nanny can look after the children at the chalet on Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve, so you can dine out without watching the clock.
- **Gifts.** Send us your list and we take care of the shopping, wrapping and delivery, to your chalet or to friends staying elsewhere in the resort, through our [lifestyle and shopping service](/services/lifestyle/).

For the days around the celebrations, see our [private experiences in Courchevel](/experiences/), from dog sledding to a dinner above the valley.

## Arriving and leaving over the holidays

The Saturdays of 19 and 26 December and 2 January are the heaviest travel days, as most chalets change guests then and the single road up from Moûtiers fills with traffic. If your chalet allows it, arriving on another day makes the journey far calmer, and a helicopter to Courchevel Altiport avoids the valley entirely when the weather allows. We always keep a chauffeured car on standby for helicopter arrivals. Our guide to [getting to Courchevel](/guides/getting-to-courchevel/) covers the routes and travel times.

## Choosing where to stay

Courchevel 1850 is where most of the festive nights out happen, with restaurants, bars and clubs close together. Families who prefer a calmer base often look at [Courchevel 1550 Village](/courchevel-1550-village/), linked to 1850 by gondola, or at [La Tania](/la-tania/), a small village in the forest between Courchevel and Méribel, with lifts towards both sides of the Trois Vallées. We help you weigh the options against how you want to spend the evenings.

## Orthodox Christmas

For families who celebrate Orthodox Christmas on 7 January, the timing works well: the French school holidays are over and the resort is quieter. We can plan the evening in the chalet with a private chef, the dishes you would like and, if you wish, a table elsewhere for the days around it.

## Timeline: what to book when

- **As soon as your dates are fixed:** the chalet or hotel, private chefs for 24, 25 and 31 December, and any New Year's Eve party at the chalet. These are reserved months ahead.
- **Next:** flights, helicopter and chauffeured transfers, timed to avoid the busiest Saturdays where possible, and ski lessons and nannies for the children, which fill several weeks ahead for the holidays.
- **Before December:** restaurant table requests for Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, menus agreed with the chef, and the decoration you would like in the chalet.
- **Early December:** your gift list, so shopping and wrapping can be done before you arrive.
- **During your stay:** anything else. We are available 24/7 in the winter season for changes and new requests.
