## How it works

1. **Tell us your dates and who is coming.** Rooms or suites, the hotel you have in mind, or what matters most: on the slopes, near the centre, a spa, a club for the children.
2. **A proposal within 24 hours.** The options that fit, each quoted for you. If the hotel you want is full, we propose the closest alternatives.
3. **Everything around the stay.** The room is booked with the hotel, and we arrange the rest: transfers, ski passes, restaurant tables and requests during your stay.
