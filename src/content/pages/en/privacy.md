## Who is responsible

The data controller is the business named above. For any question about your personal data, or to exercise your rights, write to us at the email address above.

## What we collect

**When you send a request** through Plan my stay or one of our short request forms:

- your name, your email address, your phone number if you give it, and how you prefer to be contacted;
- the details of your request: dates, village, number of adults and children and the children's ages, the services you are interested in, who the booking is for, your budget in your own words and your message; for a short request, its own details, such as the restaurant, time and number of guests, or the route and flight of a transfer;
- your agreement to this policy and when you gave it;
- the page you sent the request from, the website that brought you to us and any campaign tags in the web address, so that we know which pages and channels are useful.

**When you write to us** by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive what you choose to send.

**When you browse the site**, our host records technical data such as your IP address and browser in its logs, for security. If you accept audience measurement, Google Analytics collects data about your visit, as explained in our [cookie policy](/cookies/). Our spam protection may check your browser when you send a form.

## Why we use it, and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
| --- | --- |
| Answering your request and preparing a proposal | Steps taken at your request before a contract, art. 6(1)(b) |
| Arranging and following your stay once you book | Performance of the contract, art. 6(1)(b) |
| Following up a proposal you have not answered, with up to three messages | Our legitimate interest in answering requests well, art. 6(1)(f) |
| Keeping the site and our forms secure, and stopping spam | Our legitimate interest, art. 6(1)(f) |
| Measuring the audience of the site with Google Analytics | Your consent, art. 6(1)(a), which you can withdraw at any time |
| Keeping accounting records once a service is invoiced | Legal obligation, art. 6(1)(c) |

We do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not send newsletters.

## Who receives it

Only the people who need it:

- our team;
- the providers that run our website and our requests, acting only on our instructions: Netlify, which hosts the site and receives the forms (United States); Supabase, the database where requests are stored, with the data hosted in Paris (France); Basin, which keeps a copy of each request and sends it to our mailbox (Canada); Google, for the mailbox where requests arrive and, if you accept it, Google Analytics; and, when we use them, Resend for confirmation emails (United States), Telegram for alerts to our team, and Cloudflare for spam protection;
- when you book, the partners who provide what you asked for, such as the chalet owner or agency, the hotel, the restaurant, the driver or the chef, and only with the details they need.

We never sell or rent your data.

## Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based outside the European Union or may access data from there. Each transfer relies on a safeguard recognised by the GDPR: an adequacy decision of the European Commission, for example for Canada or for US companies certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or the Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details.

## How long we keep it

- A request that does not lead to a booking: three years after our last contact, then deleted.
- Data about a stay you booked: for as long as we work together, then five years, the legal limitation period. Invoices and accounting records: ten years, as French law requires.
- Our host's technical logs: a limited period, under its own policy.
- Google Analytics data, if you accept it: fourteen months at most.

## Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, object to its use (including our follow-up messages) and receive it in a format you can take elsewhere. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. In France you can also give instructions about what happens to your data after your death.

To exercise these rights, write to us at the email address above. We reply within one month. If we have a reasonable doubt about who is asking, we may ask you to confirm your identity.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority: 3 Place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, or online at cnil.fr.

## Security

Our forms are sent over an encrypted connection. Requests are stored in a database that visitors cannot read, and only our request system can add to it. Access to your data is limited to the people who need it to look after your request.

## Children

Our services are booked by adults. We only receive children's ages when you give them to us, to arrange things such as ski lessons or childcare.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a provider. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it last changed.
