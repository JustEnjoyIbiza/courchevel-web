## Who we are

Courchevel Luxury Concierge is a private concierge for people who spend the winter, or part of it, in Courchevel. We arrange the chalet or palace hotel, the transfers, the private chef, the ski instructor, the household staff, the nanny and the evenings out, and we stay on call while you are in the mountains.

We work for families, couples, groups of friends and companies. Requests from personal assistants, family offices and travel agents are welcome: tell us who you are booking for, and we deal with you directly.

## How we work

You have one contact from the first message to the last day of your stay. Rather than sending a catalogue, we ask a few questions about your group, your dates and what matters to you, then propose a small number of options that genuinely fit.

We do not publish prices. Every stay is different, so each service is quoted personally once we know what you need. You see the full proposal before anything is booked, and nothing is confirmed without your agreement.

## How a request is handled

1. **You get in touch** by WhatsApp, phone or email, or through the Plan my stay form.
2. **We reply within 15 minutes** between 8:00 and 22:00, to confirm we have your request and ask anything we need to know.
3. **You receive a tailored proposal within 24 hours**, with the options we recommend and a personal quote.
4. **We make the bookings** once you approve, and confirm each one in writing.
5. **We prepare your arrival:** transfers timed to your flight, the chalet stocked and staff briefed.
6. **We stay on call** throughout your stay, 24/7 during the winter season, for changes and new requests.

## The area we cover

We work across all of Courchevel: 1850, 1650 Moriond, 1550 Village, 1300 Le Praz and La Tania. Because the lift system links the whole Trois Vallées, we also arrange services in Méribel and Val Thorens, whether that is a lunch table, a guide for a day or a transfer between resorts.

## Availability

During the winter season we are available 24/7 for clients in resort. Outside urgent matters, we reply within 15 minutes from 8:00 to 22:00. The season runs from 4 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, and the busiest weeks, Christmas, New Year and February, are best planned early.

## Who we work with

We work with independent professionals and companies in and around Courchevel:

- chalet owners, letting agencies and hotels
- private chefs, sommeliers and catering teams
- ski schools, private instructors and certified mountain guides
- licensed chauffeur companies and helicopter and aviation operators
- nannies, butlers, housekeepers and wellness practitioners

We do not let property, drive clients or fly aircraft ourselves. Chalets are booked with their owners, agencies or hotels, road transfers are carried out by licensed chauffeur partners, and flights by licensed operators. Our role is to choose the right people, brief them carefully and make sure everything fits together. Where something depends on others, such as a club table on New Year's Eve, we request it and tell you plainly when it is confirmed.

## Discretion

Privacy is part of the service. We share with each provider only what they need to do their part, and we never discuss clients or their plans with anyone else. If you prefer that we use a first name or an agreed reference with suppliers, we will.

## Plan your stay

Tell us your dates, your village if you have one in mind, and who is coming. We will reply within 15 minutes during our hours and send a tailored proposal within 24 hours.
