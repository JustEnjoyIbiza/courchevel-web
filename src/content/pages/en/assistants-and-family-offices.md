## How we work with assistants and family offices

Many Courchevel stays are not booked by the person who will be skiing. They are prepared by a personal assistant, a family office, a travel agent or someone in a private office, often while handling several other trips at once. We work with you directly, from the first request to the last day in the mountains.

You have one contact throughout. Rather than sending a catalogue, we ask a few precise questions, then propose a small number of options that fit the principal's habits and the group travelling. Nothing is booked until you approve the proposal, and each booking is confirmed in writing. See [about us](/about/) for how a request is handled step by step.

## Discretion and personal data

We share with each supplier only what they need to deliver their part of the stay. A chauffeur needs a flight number and a name board; a chef needs dietary requirements and the number of guests; neither needs the rest of the itinerary.

Many clients and their assistants prefer that we use a first name or an agreed reference, and we are happy to work that way. Tell us your requirements for confidentiality before we start, including how documents should be shared and who else may be copied in, and we follow them with every supplier. Personal data is handled in line with the GDPR and used only to organise the stay.

## Written confirmations and changes at short notice

Every booking is confirmed in writing, and so is every change. If a flight moves, a guest is added or dinner needs to be cancelled, you receive a short message saying what has changed and who has been told.

Plans for principals change often, sometimes on the day itself. We reply within 15 minutes between 8:00 and 22:00, and during the winter season we are on call 24/7. When something cannot be changed, we say so straight away and suggest the closest alternative.

## Several chalets, several households

Some families take more than one property: a main chalet, a second one for friends or staff, rooms in a hotel for a short visit. We coordinate the bookings across them, keep arrivals, transfers and chefs aligned, and make sure each property has what it needs before the guests arrive.

We are not a letting agency and do not hold rent: the chalet owner, agency or hotel remains the contractual partner for the accommodation. For live-in help, our [household staff service](/services/staff/) covers butlers, chalet managers, housekeeping and serving staff, and our [childcare service](/services/childcare/) covers nannies. For requests during the stay, such as gifts, deliveries, reservations or a forgotten item, see our [lifestyle service](/services/lifestyle/).

## WhatsApp groups and how we stay in touch

We work by WhatsApp, phone or email, whichever suits you. For larger stays, a WhatsApp group with you, the principal's staff and, where useful, the chalet team keeps everyone informed without long email chains. If you prefer that the principal is never contacted directly, tell us and all messages go through you.

## Invoicing

Every service is quoted personally, and you see the full proposal before anything is booked. Tell us your requirements for invoicing, for example which entity is invoiced, what reference should appear or who approves each booking, and we take them into account from the first proposal.

## What to send us

A first request does not need to be complete. These details help us prepare an accurate proposal:

- **Dates and village**, or the area you are considering, such as [Courchevel 1550 Village](/courchevel-1550-village/) or [La Tania](/la-tania/)
- **Who is travelling:** number of adults and children, ages of the children, and any staff travelling with the family
- **Travel plans:** airport, private flight or train, and arrival times once known (see [getting to Courchevel](/guides/getting-to-courchevel/))
- **Preferences:** chalet or hotel, ski levels, dietary requirements, allergies and any regular routines
- **Confidentiality and invoicing:** how the principal should be named, who may be contacted and how invoices should be issued

Send what you have by WhatsApp, phone or email, and we will ask for the rest. You receive a tailored proposal within 24 hours.
