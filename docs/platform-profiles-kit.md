## 2. Reviews (Google first)

- **Links:** until launch (24 Nov) the domain still shows the old site, so use Google's own link:
  `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJPRJmAL2Kv4ARuPyRohUWW9M`.
  From launch, the short links `courchevelluxuryconcierge.com/review` (EN) and `/avis` (FR) open the same box
  (already set in `netlify.toml`); use them in messages and as a QR code on the chalet welcome card.
- **Who and when:** every past client this week (aim for 30 reviews by Christmas), then every client 24 to 48
  hours after departure.
- **Never** offer anything in exchange, never ask only happy clients, never write reviews yourself: Google removes
  them and can suspend the profile.
- **Reply to every review within 48 hours**, in the reviewer's language, naming the service and place; never
  mention private details (names of guests, chalet, dates) the client did not mention.
- **Existing reviews:** several mention July visits and are in Spanish. If they relate to work outside Courchevel,
  Google may treat them as not matching this business. Reviews should describe Courchevel stays.
- **On the website:** the homepage shows the rating from `profiles.google.rating` in `src/data/business.ts`
  with its date (update it when the count changes; the launch check fails after 45 days). With a Google Places API
  key (`GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY`) the rating is read live at each build instead.
