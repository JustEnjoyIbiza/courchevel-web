## 3. AI recommendation readiness (GEO)

Done on the site:
- Crawlable by every AI crawler (robots.txt), `llms.txt` generated from site data (now with planning pages and village altitudes).
- Every page answers first in 2–3 sentences, then facts, FAQ, related pages, a visible "Last updated" date.
- 62 FAQ blocks, all marked up, all visible on the page (Google requirement).
- Business entity: same name, phone, description everywhere; `knowsAbout` lists the 12 services; contact point in English and French.
- Quotable, sourced facts (altiport, transfer times, season dates, heli-skiing law, ESF ages) that AI assistants prefer to cite.

Still missing (needs you):
- **Founder** (name, photo, languages, year started) → Person schema and About page. Biggest single E-E-A-T gain.
- **Reviews** (Google first). Competitors show 4.5–4.9 ratings; AI assistants weigh them heavily.
- **Profiles for `sameAs`**: Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Trustpilot, Facebook (only Instagram today).
- **Domain email** instead of gmail (trust signal, and needed for sending lead emails).

## 4. Internal links

- No orphan pages; every page within 2 clicks of the homepage.
- Homepage now has a Guides section and a seasonal Christmas & New Year line.
- Experiences link to the services each one uses; every FAQ topic links to its related page.
- New pages link to the previously weak pages (About, FAQ, 1550 Village, La Tania, Lifestyle, Getting to Courchevel).
- Villages hub: comparison table with altitude, Courchevel villages and the rest of the Trois Vallées.
- Every page now has at least 3 contextual links pointing to it; no French page links to English or vice versa.

## 5. Text correction

- English: British spelling, "Trois Vallées" everywhere, 24-hour times, durations written out in prose, the repeated reply promise varied, overclaims removed ("priority tables", "certified multilingual nannies").
- French: ~400 apostrophes and typography fixed, anglicisms replaced ("chalet manager" → "intendant", "lifestyle" → "art de vivre"), experience names translated (Semaine en famille, Noël et Nouvel An, Séminaire en montagne), legal wording tightened (we never let property or drive clients).
- Facts aligned between pages (Chambéry drive time, altiport and private jets), unverified claims removed (ice circuit "one of the highest in Europe").
- French confirmation email now uses French colon spacing.

## 6. Responsiveness

Tested at 320, 390, 430, 768, 1024 and 1920 px on every template and new page:
- Fixed: buttons forcing 10–30 px of sideways scroll on small phones (they now wrap below 640 px), long French headings, wide tables (scroll inside themselves), small tap targets (language switch, logo, breadcrumbs, FAQ questions, table and sidebar links), logo tagline size.
- Remaining by design: wide comparison tables scroll horizontally inside their box on 320 px phones.

1. **Google Business Profile** (service-area until the address is ready; verify in November). Same name, phone, description.
2. **Reviews**: ask every past client for a Google review; reply to each within 48 hours.
3. **courchevel.com listing** (tourist office "useful services / concierge", via Apidae).
4. **Trustpilot** profile (cited for Nightfall).
5. **Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Tripadvisor**.

7. **Monthly AI prompt test** (20 prompts, EN and FR) to measure progress and find the next outreach targets.
