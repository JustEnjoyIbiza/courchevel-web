# Luxury mountain concierge benchmark (23 October 2026)

Eight high-end mountain sites reviewed (read-only) for design and page ideas to adapt, not copy:
Bramble Ski (brambleski.com), Leo Trippi (leotrippi.com), Verbier Exclusive (verbierexclusive.com),
Le Collectionist (lecollectionist.com), Consensio Chalets (consensiochalets.co.uk),
Haute Retreats (hauteretreats.com), MX Zermatt (mxzermatt.com), Ultimate Luxury Chalets
(ultimateluxurychalets.com). Earlier Courchevel-only review: `competitor-review-2026-10-22.md`.

## Best idea per site

| Site | Idea worth adapting |
|---|---|
| Bramble Ski | Each service tier shown as "included" vs "on request", without prices |
| Leo Trippi | Neighbourhood guidance phrased as who each area suits |
| Verbier Exclusive | Named founder with a short bio; a "safe hands" team section |
| Le Collectionist | Sample experiences told as short stories, not service lists |
| Consensio | Concierge services grouped by stage of the stay |
| Haute Retreats | Three-step process and a low-commitment "tailored selection" CTA |
| MX Zermatt | Services split by season (and a warning: no names, no CTA = hollow) |
| Ultimate Luxury Chalets | Quick-facts block (altitude, transfer times); also a list of clutter to avoid |

## Adopted on 23 October

- Full-screen photo hero on the homepage, centred (done earlier the same day).
- Photo header on every service, village and guide page and on the Services, Villages, Guides and
  Experiences hubs; the direct answer follows as a large serif statement with the two CTAs.
  Childcare and Val Thorens got photos (Val Thorens uses a generic gondola photo, alt text says so).
- Services hub: "Your stay, stage by stage" (before arrival, arrival and departure, on the mountain,
  evenings, throughout the stay), each linking to the matching services.
- Closing band on every page: italic accent line ("We take care of the rest.") and WhatsApp, phone
  and email with availability.

## Already on the site before the review

One repeated CTA wording, three-step "how it works", FAQ in `<details>`, quick facts per page,
who each village suits, the example week in 1850, EN/FR with hreflang, short no-JS form.

## Needs the owner (highest impact remaining)

1. A named founder or concierge with portrait and short bio (About page and homepage).
2. Real anonymised requests or example stories approved by the owner.
3. Real photos: landscape shots for desktop heroes, chalet interiors, chef, arrivals.
4. The live Google rating (Places API key).

## Rejected (break our rules)

Press/award strips and statistics without proof, "from" prices and budget bands, pop-ups, modals
and drawers, icon grids, carousels and hero video autoplay without a still fallback, count-up
counters (read 0 without JavaScript), date-search widgets, rounded buttons and shadows.
