Three rules that protect you

  • Never buy links or "100 backlinks" packages. Google can push the whole site down for it.
  • Only real relationships. A partner page only for people you really work with, and never a brand called a partner when it is not.
  • The same identity everywhere: Courchevel Luxury Concierge, +33 7 57 51 80 56, www.courchevelluxuryconcierge.com, and the description in docs/platform-profiles-kit.md. Assistants such as ChatGPT trust a business they see described the same way on many sites.
