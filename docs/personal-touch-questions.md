# Personal-touch questions for the owner

Written 23 October 2026. Each page of the new site is accurate, but it reads like a well-researched
guide. What it lacks is **you**: what you have seen, done and learnt in Courchevel. Google calls this
"experience" (the first E in E-E-A-T), AI assistants quote it, and clients trust it more than anything
we can write.

This is **not a rewrite**. Each answer becomes one or two sentences, or a short example, in your voice.

## How to answer

- **Voice notes are perfect.** WhatsApp voice notes, rough notes, bullet points; English, French or
  Spanish. One minute per question is plenty. Quote the question number (e.g. "S3") at the start.
- **Start at the top.** The list is in priority order: About, homepage, then the services and villages
  that bring the most enquiries. Stop whenever you like; every answer helps.
- **Skip anything you don't want to share.** "Skip" is a fine answer.
- **Real stories, no names.** Tell us what actually happened. We anonymise everything ("a family of
  six from London", "a client last February") and never name clients, chalets, staff or partners
  without your written OK.
- **No prices.** Even in examples. Say "a lot", "more than people expect", or "it depends on…".

What we do with the answers: weave them into the pages in your voice, keep every fact you give us
exactly as you say it, run all the quality checks again, and show you the changes before they go live.

The About page is still waiting for founder facts (name, photo, languages, year started: see
`owner-checklist.md`). These questions are about the story behind them.

- **A1.** How did you start working in Courchevel, and why concierge work?
- **A2.** What is a typical day for you in January? And on 31 December?
- **A3.** What do you do differently from a hotel concierge or a big agency? An example helps more
  than an adjective.
- **A4.** Is there a request you are proud of having solved, the one you tell friends about?
  (Anonymised.)
- **A5.** Who is on the team in winter (roles, not names if you prefer), and which languages do you
