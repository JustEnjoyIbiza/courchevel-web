# Competitor review and persuasion scores (22 October 2026)

Six Courchevel concierge websites reviewed (read-only), compared with our rebuild.
Scores are expert estimates from page structure and typical luxury-buyer behaviour, not analytics.
Engagement = how long and how deep a high-net-worth prospect would explore (10 = many pages, several minutes).
Persuasion = how well the site convinces them to enquire here rather than with a rival.

## Our selling points and how often they repeat (homepage)

- "Plan my stay" / enquire: 7 links; WhatsApp: 4; phone: 1 (plus the sticky mobile bar).
- "One contact": hero, services title, About block, final CTA.
- "Reply within 15 minutes": proof strip, How it works, final CTA, every page's CTA band.
- "Quoted personally, not from a price list": hero and every service page.
- Same CTA band on every service, village and guide page. Consistent, not pushy; no pop-ups.

## What none of the competitors do (our openings)

1. Real, verifiable Google reviews on the page (ours: 5.0★ live from Google once the API key is set).
2. A named, photographed Courchevel concierge (only White Management names a founder).
3. A sample week or itinerary showing how a stay is put together.
4. A clear comparison with hotel concierges (we have it: `/guides/private-concierge-vs-hotel-concierge/`).
5. A page for assistants and family offices (we have it: `/assistants-and-family-offices/`).
